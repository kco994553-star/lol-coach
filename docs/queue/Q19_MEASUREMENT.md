# Q19 explicit numerical comparison

Authority: USER_QUEUE_V1.6_2026-10-11. The Main-owned `contracts/expert-v1.md` and `contracts/expert-v1-amendment.md` at `6471946` define the optional criterion and delegate comparison to this additive module. Existing artifacts, movement contracts, validators, approvals, stores, servers and frozen payloads are preserved.

`coach_v1.expert_measurement.compare_claim(claim, movement_data, test_mode=False)` is pure. It returns exactly `{status, reasons, statistic}`. Status is AGREES, DISAGREES, INSUFFICIENT or UNMEASURABLE; reasons are safe enum strings. It does not fetch, write, approve, parse prose, classify an utterance's factual truth or infer an unprovided distance radius.

The bridge validates the full expert artifact. This module independently checks the numerical criterion's exact fields: `kind=MOVEMENT_MEAN_DISTANCE`, 64-hex `cohort_id`, numeric `patch`, standard Riot `tier`, nonnegative integer `minute`, `operator=LE|GE`, finite nonnegative `threshold`, credential-free HTTPS `criterion_source_url`, bounded original `rationale`, and `origin=MANUAL|REVIEWED`. A measurable=false claim or null criterion is UNMEASURABLE. Malformed or unsupported criteria also remain UNMEASURABLE. Origin is a caller assertion; the comparator never creates approval.

A known canonical champion, one of the five positions and a known EARLY/MID/LATE stage must exactly match the selected cohort. UNKNOWN or mismatched scope is INSUFFICIENT. Criterion patch, tier and minute must match exactly; there is no opponent, role, patch, tier, time or cohort fallback. A dataset whose source.patch=null may contain separate patch cohorts, but only the explicitly selected cohort's patch is compared. Known claim.patch_range must be an ASCII numeric major.minor patch or an inclusive major.minor-major.minor range containing criterion.patch; numeric components are compared as integer pairs, not lexical strings. Malformed, reversed or out-of-range scope is INSUFFICIENT with statistic=null. UNKNOWN may support only the explicit numerical comparison and always adds CLAIM_PATCH_UNKNOWN_COMPARISON_ONLY; it does not establish the video's patch or tactical applicability.

Movement data passes `validate_movement_dataset` first. Provider/sample-kind parity, derived formula, phase condition signature, VERIFIED phase lineage, chronological/minute-bin provenance, aggregate counts and Student-t CI validity remain the movement validator's responsibility. A minute-only cohort cannot acquire a stage from its minute or the utterance. Missing, invalid or nonmatching data is INSUFFICIENT, with statistic=null. At the requested minute, n>=2 and the movement precision policy's visible CI are required. Hidden or missing intervals never become zero, false, disagreement or evidence of absence.

| Criterion | AGREES | DISAGREES | INSUFFICIENT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LE | CI high <= threshold | CI low > threshold | The interval crosses the boundary |
| GE | CI low >= threshold | CI high < threshold | The interval crosses the boundary |

A visible interval crossing the criterion retains its statistic as inconclusive evidence. Exact inclusive boundaries are supported. No interval is rounded or clipped for comparison.

Real aggregates are labeled `evidence_kind=REAL_AGGREGATE`. Synthetic data is withheld in normal mode; only literal `test_mode=True` permits it, always with `evidence_kind=TEST` and `SYNTHETIC_TEST_NOT_REAL_EVIDENCE`. Tests author synthetic fixtures even when exercising REAL metadata gates; they do not publish real receipts or measure coaching performance.

When available, statistic exact fields are `dataset_sha256`, `cohort_id`, `champion`, `position`, `stage`, `patch`, `tier`, `minute`, `condition_signature`, `source`, `formula`, `n`, `mean_distance`, `ci95`, `criterion`, `limitations`, and `evidence_kind`. The canonical digest covers the complete validated dataset. The selected point and caller criterion remain exact; source and all limitations are copied. Source metadata above 32KiB is withheld with SOURCE_METADATA_TOO_LARGE, bounding the returned provenance. Raw datasets, participant/frame objects, coordinates, match IDs and hashed-match lists are not returned. Input claim/data are never mutated.

The attached `NUMERICAL_COMPARISON_NOT_TACTICAL_VALIDATION_OR_APPROVAL` limitation applies to every statistic. Agreement means only that the specified descriptive aggregate meets the supplied numerical boundary with this interval. It does not prove that an expert statement is true, the underlying speech was correctly summarized, a formation was observed, a tactic causes success, or a rule is approved. The movement distance remains the coordinate-wise known-allies median proxy with its existing sampling, tier, selection and quantization limitations.
