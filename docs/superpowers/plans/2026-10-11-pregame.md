## Review Focus
- Manual self champion conflicts with allied role or ambiguous positions: reject contradiction/retain candidates.
- Partial rune/order data cannot satisfy complete required information.
- New EXPLORATORY/rejected/deleted current knowledge expires an old approved plan.
- Conflicting source/profile/outlook must withhold conclusions, without arbitrary scoring.
- Network response loss, stale UI response and restart must preserve the exact input and immutable revisions.
