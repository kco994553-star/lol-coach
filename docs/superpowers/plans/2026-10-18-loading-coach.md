## Global Constraints
- Existing Frozen/advice7/AUTO30HOLD20/raw/holds/failures/Windows/manual/privacy/video limits preserved.
- Observations, standard assumptions, preferences and knowledge confidence separate; N0/unmeasured comprehension unchanged.
- No invented odds/damage/minion/time/item threshold; unknown never contested/safe.
- Main owns interface and integration. No new video/Gemini/game/paid use. Free text original source research only.

## Review Focus
- Different/missing support, unknown assignment or reversed input order must not inherit a four-person matchup.
- Guide dates/patch namespaces and self-claimed expertise must stay visible; stale thresholds never become current facts.
- UNKNOWN override must revoke only the affected advantage/action; no risk preference promotion.
- CC taken must compare remaining tools rather than unconditionally retreat; shield cannot become between-trade healing.
- Growth risk event cannot silently become total reversal, exact time or fixed universal3item spike.
