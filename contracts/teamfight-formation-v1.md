## Input/output and applicability

loading-formation-v1 consumes the normalized ten picks, own champion, patch and existing
catalog/knowledge. Player planboard adds fight.formations; old fields stay compatible.
Requires opposing Caitlyn/Kaisa, five known picks per side, patch agreement and a
source-linked FRONTLINE candidate on each side. Other combinations explicitly unsupported.
No exact matchup winner or ideal coordinates proven. FRONTLINE/Assassin/Fighter tags only
assign explanatory candidate roles; no guarantee of tanking, flank feasibility or victory.
Array order cannot change geometry. Risk preference does not change this model or strength.

Output has supported/reason, origin=ILLUSTRATIVE_HYPOTHESIS, actual_enemy_positions=null;
two scenes FRONT_CONNECTED/FLANK_ISOLATE, ten actual-input actors (team/self/role/three
positions), three steps, purpose/reason/conditions/failure, winner=UNDETERMINED. Coordinates
are purely illustrative. The renderer owns only selected scene/step/actor/zoom. One state
updates both normal and enlarged diagrams and the action explanation. Portrait click
explains an actor; it never moves a champion or infers a safe route. Both teams have
labelled assumed positions, unlike the old real-map view which hides unknown enemies.

Frontal plan: Caitlyn attacks reachable targets while allied frontline/control/protection
remain connected; not a claim all frontal5v5 favor this team. Avoid tight stacking, respect
flanks and remaining enemy tools. Flank plan: Kai'Sa may follow a marked isolated target
when allied access/follow-up and remaining defensive means permit; scattering is not a
general best play. If allies are isolated first, protection remains or escape is missing,
do not force the dive. A flank advantage is not an all-in/kill/chase verdict.
